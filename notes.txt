Google Docs: collaborative editing system.

1.  a)Functional requirements:
able to perform CRUD operations, multiple user concurrency (upto 100 users) in a single file while updating, updates should be visible to all, cursor position to avoid conflicts, 100kb<=document size<100gb or more .
    b)Non-Functional requirements:
millions of documents, latency <=200ms, document should stay consistent amongst all its local users.

2. API/ Database designs:
POST/api/documents
GET/api/documents/{documentId}
PUT/api/documents/{documentId}
Ws/api/documents/{documentId}/collaborate
